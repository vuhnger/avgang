# Avgang

Live departures from your Oslo stops in the menu bar, from Entur.

Download the latest `.dmg` from [releases](https://github.com/vuhnger/avgang/releases/latest) and drag it to Applications.
The first launch says Apple could not verify Avgang is free of malware, because the app is not notarized. Click Done, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway next to Avgang and confirm.
Add your stops in Settings, or allow location access to see the stops nearest you.
